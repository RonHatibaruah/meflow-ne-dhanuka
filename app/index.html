MediFlow-NE: Interactive System Tutorial & User Guide

Understanding the AI Supply Chain Platform for G.R. Dhanuka Group (NESFIC-I-09)

The Regional Problem (NESFIC-I-09): Northeast India faces severe logistics bottlenecks due to seasonal Brahmaputra floods and landslides cutting key highway corridors (NH-6 to Barak Valley, NH-29 to Nagaland). Central warehouses in Guwahati lack visibility into peripheral chemists, causing 25–34% stockouts of life-saving medicines during monsoons, while urban stockists face 6–9% near-expiry wastage.
1 Interactive GIS Logistics Radar

Explore the interactive map of Northeast India. Click any hub (Guwahati, Silchar, Dibrugarh, Tezpur) to inspect lead-time variations, live flood hazard warnings along NH-6, and stock runway for vital medicines.

2 AI Forecast & Scenario Studio

Powered by Temporal Fusion Transformers (TFT). Adjust the Monsoon Severity, Epidemic Outbreak, and Transit Delay sliders to watch the demand forecast curve dynamically adapt in real-time.

3 FEFO Expiry Arbitrage Matrix

The platform automatically detects slow-moving batches nearing 6 months of expiry. Click "Rebalance" in the table to dynamically simulate transferring them to high-velocity emergency wards.

4 Rural Chemist WhatsApp Bot

Type a test message (e.g., "Stock Cefixime: 5 packs left") in the mobile chat panel to experience how rural pharmacies with spotty 2G internet can log stock and confirm emergency replenishments in under 30 seconds.

5 Admin Command & Control Center

Click "Admin Panel (Login)" in the top header. Use demo credentials (admin@dhanuka.com / dhanuka2026) to unlock route hazard management, buffer rule tuning, stockist ERP sync health, and GxP audit logs.
